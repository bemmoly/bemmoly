A page opens in the doc editor. The bar over it shows where the page sits in its space, its
status as a menu of the moves the review flow allows (asking for review picks the reviewers
first; publishing and archiving need publish rights), whether everything is saved, who else is
on the page, a star, Share and a menu to copy the link, move the page and move it to the trash
with Undo. The title is edited where it stands and Enter carries on into the body, which is
edited live with everyone who has the page open. Labels, the owner and the reviewers show as
chips above the title, and a line under it gives the owner, the dates and the reading time. An
outline of the page's headings follows the text as people write, marks the section being read
and scrolls to a section on a click; it sits beside the body on wide screens and in the About
panel otherwise. The About panel lists the status, owner, reviewers and labels (all three edited
in place), the length, when the page was made and changed, and who wrote it. A page in the
trash opens with a Restore banner, an archived page reads as it was left with a way back to
draft, and offline, read-only, missing and no-access pages each say so. ⌘S answers that saving
is automatic, and ⌘. shows or hides the About panel. Below 1280px the panel opens over the body
and on phones the space's pages fold into a drawer. No configuration change.
