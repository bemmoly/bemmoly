The doc editor's side panel gains Comments and Linked work tabs, and the page's More menu gains
Version history and Export.

Select words in a page and choose Comment (or press ⌘⌥M, Ctrl+Alt+M elsewhere) to start a thread
on them. Commented text is highlighted in the page; clicking it opens its thread, and focusing a
thread scrolls the page to its text. Threads list in reading order with replies, @mentions, edit
and delete for the author, and resolve or reopen, with open and resolved threads kept apart. A
thread whose words were edited away keeps its quote, marked "Text changed". A thread that
carries a suggested fix offers Apply fix; others do not.

Version history lists a page's versions, saves a named one, compares any version with the page
now or with the version before it, and restores one after saying what happens: everyone with the
page open sees the change, and the restore is kept as a new version. The compare marks added and
removed blocks, moved blocks linked to the place they left, inserted, deleted and reformatted
words, and single table cells, and folds long unchanged stretches.

Linked work shows the issues a page references, the records that reference it and the pages
that link to it. Pages export as Markdown or HTML, with their subpages as a zip for people who
may export the space, and the space sidebar's Import takes Markdown files or folders, or the
pages of a Confluence space export.
