Bemmoly People · product proposal

Day one, already set up

People keeps the one record of everyone at your company: who they are, where they work, who they report to and what they're allowed to see. Every other Bemmoly product reads it. So when someone joins, moves or is promoted, the change is made once and the laptop, the accounts, the goals and the letter follow on their own. It's its own product. Use it alone, or with the others.

01The problem today

A person's record is copied into payroll, the IT directory, the laptop spreadsheet and three approval chains. When it changes, someone retypes it, or nobody does. New hires feel it first: the job they said yes to arrives without a laptop, an account or a plan.

12%of employees strongly agree their organisation does a great job of onboardingGallup, 2017
44 dayson average for a new hire to decide whether a job is right for themBambooHR survey, 2023
33%of workers started a job without their computer, phone or security access set upOfficeTeam (Robert Half), 2015
24%of HR leaders believe they get the most value out of their HR technologyGartner, via SHRM, 2024
One person, many copies

HR, payroll, IT and finance each keep their own list. A change of manager or address reaches some of them, late, by hand. Nobody is sure which copy is right.

Sapient Insights put the average organisation at about 16 HR applications in 2021.
Onboarding is a relay of emails

People asks IT for a laptop, IT asks the manager which apps, the manager forgets the plan. The hire learns what was missed when they sit down on day one.

OfficeTeam, 2015: a third had their tech not set up when they arrived.
Time off is approved blind

A manager sees one request at a time, with no view of who else is away or what's due that week. The clash shows up when a deadline slips.

Changes are tickets, not events

A promotion means a letter, a payroll change, new access and a new expense grade, each done by a different person. Leavers keep access because nobody owns the last step.

Global teams don't fit one form

India needs a PAN and a UAN, Germany a tax ID and a health insurer, the US an SSN. Leave rules and holidays differ per country. Rigid tools fork into one system per region.

Simple questions need an analyst

"Why did attrition rise in Sales?" becomes an export, a pivot table and a week. Salaries leak into spreadsheets on the way, because the tool can't answer safely.

Gartner, 2024: nearly half of HR leaders say HR tech has hurt HR's reputation.

03Market leaders, and what they teach us

HR software splits into enterprise suites, unified platforms that join HR and IT, modern mid-market HRIS, global employment platforms and India's payroll-first HRMS. This is an internal benchmark. Product screens never name these products.

Workday HCMEnterprise · global

The system of record for large companies: deep business processes, security groups and reporting, and since 2025 a set of AI agents, including a self-service agent for employees and managers.

Configurable business processes for every lifecycle event, with strong audit.
Long, partner-led rollouts. Changing a process usually needs a specialist.
RipplingUnified HR and IT

An employee graph holds role, devices and apps. Groups built from attributes provision apps automatically, so a new hire gets the right apps and a device from one onboarding flow.

The closest to "change it once, everything follows" for HR, IT and apps.
The magic works inside its own modules, each priced separately. Reviewers find setup complex for small teams.
HiBob and PersonioModern mid-market

Friendly HRIS for growing companies. HiBob is known for its culture features and flexible org charts with dotted lines. Personio for absence management with substitutes and rule-based workflows.

A self-service experience employees actually like, and quick setup.
Workflows stay inside HR. Setting up IT and other teams still means integrations.
BambooHRSmall business · US

A clean employee record, time off, onboarding checklists and e-signatures, with payroll in the US and a large integration marketplace.

Simple to learn. Strong onboarding research and templates.
Onboarding is mostly a checklist. Other teams act on emails and tasks.
Deel and RemoteGlobal employment

Employer of record and contractor platforms that added a global HRIS, offered free or at low cost, covering employees, contractors and EOR staff in many countries.

Country compliance knowledge: contracts, leave and local benefits.
The HRIS is a door to paid employment services. Customisation is limited.
Darwinbox, Keka, Zoho People, greytHRIndia and value

Payroll and statutory compliance first (PF, ESI, PT, TDS), with leave, attendance and self-service. Darwinbox serves enterprises; Keka, Zoho People and greytHR serve growing companies.

Local payroll done properly, at low prices per person.
Closed source. Customisation beyond forms often needs the vendor.

04Where Bemmoly does better

We take the leaders' best ideas (the employee graph, substitutes on leave, country knowledge) and add what only an open platform with many products on one kernel can do.

One record, read by every product

People is the source of truth for the kernel's person directory. Assets, Access, Expenses and the rest read it directly, so a change of manager or office is made once. Without People installed, the kernel keeps a simple people list.

Leaders: one unified suite does this for its own modules; the rest sync through integrations.
Onboarding is one journey, across products

Hiring hands over the signed offer. People fans the journey out: Assets ships the laptop, Access queues the apps, Performance drafts 30/60/90 goals, Helpdesk opens the setup tasks. Each step shows its owner and its status in one view.

Leaders: unified suites provision apps and devices; most others hand IT a checklist.
Time off that knows the work

A request shows who else is away, the team's coverage rule and what's due in Work that week. AI points out the clash and offers fixes; the person still decides.

New: leaders show calendars and substitutes, not the deadlines in the team's work.
Lifecycle events with a preview

A promotion, transfer or exit shows exactly what will change, in which product and for whom, before anyone approves it. The letter is drafted from the same change.

Leaders: enterprise suites have business processes, configured by specialists, with no preview across products.
Answers that respect who's asking

Ask a question in plain words and get numbers with their query and sources. The model only sees what the asker may see, and groups under five people are never broken down.

New: most HR analytics assistants run in the vendor's cloud on their model.
Payroll as a connector, and no seat price

Payroll is statutory work that changes every year in every country. We send pay changes to the payroll provider you already trust, and bring payslips back. Everyone, including contractors, is in People at no cost.

Leaders: payroll is bundled, which is what makes leaving hard, and every person is a seat.

05The experience

Eight screens, drawn in Bemmoly's frame and tokens. People has its own shell; other products are reached through the product switcher. Click the tabs, or the highlighted controls inside a screen to follow the flow. The appearance switch at the top right flips every screen between light and dark.

06Make it yours

Every company has its own shape: entities in different countries, its own leave rules, its own idea of who may see a salary. People bends to it from People / Settings, without writing code, and each part can be exported as a file to review like code.

Entities and countries

Each legal entity has a country, currency, work week, holiday calendars per location and its own sections of fields. India, Germany and US templates ship; the community adds more.

Fields and sections

Typed custom fields on the person, grouped in sections that appear per entity, team or worker type (employee, contractor, intern). Fields are usable in rules, workflows and analytics.

Visibility per field

For each field: who can see it and who can edit it (the person, everyone, manager, manager's manager, People, Finance or a custom group). A "who can see" check answers it for any field and person.

Leave policies

Written as sentences: accrual, pro-rating, carry-over and expiry, half days, sick-note rules, approvals and team coverage. Every edit is versioned and previewed against real people.

Lifecycle workflows

Joiner, mover, promotion, leave of absence and exit, built on the kernel's shared workflow engine: steps, approvals, and actions in other products, each with a fallback when that product isn't installed.

Templates and connectors

Letter templates with fields from the record, onboarding journeys per role or country, and connectors for payroll, e-signature, background checks and imports. Each connector is a plug-in with a public interface.

08How it connects

People is its own product, on Bemmoly's kernel: one sign-in, the shared person directory (People becomes its source of truth when installed), the shared workflow and approval engine, typed fields, AI, audit and backups. Cross-product steps open or call the other product, with a person's confirmation, and each has a fallback when that product isn't installed.

Other Bemmoly products

  • Hiring: a signed offer creates the person and starts the journey. Without Hiring, People adds the person by hand or import.
  • Assets: ships and collects laptops and badges for joiners and leavers. Without it, a task for IT.
  • Access: birthright apps by role and location, and revocation on exit at the exact hour. Without it, a checklist per app.
  • Performance: 30/60/90 goals, probation reviews, promotion history. Without it, goals stay a note on the journey.
  • Helpdesk: setup tasks, and HR questions that AI can't answer. Without it, tasks in People.
  • Time, Expenses, Licences: read holidays, managers, grades, cost centres and seats from the record.
  • Work and Docs: starter issues, milestones for coverage, the onboarding guide.

Core objects

  • person: the kernel's directory entry, owned by People when installed
  • employment: entity, job, grade, manager, location, dates, worker type, with full history
  • org_unit and position: teams and roles, including open ones
  • leave_policy, holiday_calendar, leave_request, balance_entry: a ledger, so every balance can be explained
  • lifecycle_event: a run of the shared workflow engine, with its change set
  • document and letter_template: files, signatures, expiry dates

10Open questions

  1. Do we build payroll?Recommendation: no. Payroll is statutory work that changes every year in every country (PF, ESI and TDS in India, Lohnsteuer and social insurance in Germany, state taxes in the US). Getting it wrong hurts people's pay. We send changes to payroll providers through connectors and bring payslips and tax forms back.
  2. Who owns attendance and shifts: People or Time?Recommendation: Time owns hours, shifts and attendance. People owns leave and holidays, and Time reads them.
  3. When does a candidate become a person?Recommendation: when the offer is signed in Hiring. People creates a pre-hire record with no account; Access turns accounts on at the start time.
  4. Is People or Access the source of truth for identity?Recommendation: People says who exists and what their attributes are. Access derives accounts and groups from those attributes, and never edits them.
  5. Built-in e-signature or a connector?Recommendation: a built-in simple signature with an audit trail for letters and policies, and connectors for qualified signatures where a country requires them.
  6. How do we store identity numbers?Recommendation: field-level encryption with keys per workspace, masked by default, never sent to a model, and every reveal logged.
