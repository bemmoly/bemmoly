Calm: sidebar and copilot panel
Neutral, quiet, medium density. One left sidebar holds both spaces. A copilot panel that you can close sits on the right and adapts to whatever you're viewing. Geist plus Geist Mono for IDs. Most familiar for mixed teams.

We're moving session handling out of the monolith into a standalone auth service. This doc covers token rotation, migration order, and rollback.
Rollback plan
If error rates exceed 0.5% after cutover, we flip the feature flag back to the monolith. Sessions issued by the new service stay valid for 15 minutes, so users aren't logged out. On-call follows the runbook linked in PLT-221.
